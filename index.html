Our Story

Aromatic Coffee was born from a deep passion for specialty coffee. We started as a small local roaster with the dream of sharing the exceptional flavors of Guatemalan coffees with the world. Today, we work hand in hand with local producers, guaranteeing premium quality and fair practices. Each cup tells a story of dedication, tradition, and love for coffee.
